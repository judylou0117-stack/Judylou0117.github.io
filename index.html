01 / ABOUT ME 关于我

The person behind the projects.

关于我：我的性格、学习方式，以及想做事的初衷。

I like getting the numbers right.
And asking what they’re for.

Accounting taught me to look closely. AI is teaching me to imagine differently. Somewhere between a spreadsheet and a new idea, I’m finding the kind of work I want to do.

会计让我习惯把细节弄清楚，AI 则让我愿意追问：这件事，有没有另一种做法？我喜欢新鲜事物，也愿意陪一个想法走过最不新鲜的阶段——反复练习、修改、再试一次。

I care about useful answers: a clearer decision, a little less repetitive work, or one less thing for someone to worry about. My direction is still growing. The wish to help people is already there.

我在意的，是一个想法落到生活里之后，能不能让人少一点困惑、多一点从容。未来不一定只在金融里，但我希望做的事，总能帮到具体的人。

04 / A FUTURE I CARE ABOUT 梦想与想法

Useful technology, more confident choices, and room for kindness.

✳

What if doing well
helped someone else
do better?

I’d like to grow PauseBeforeBuy beyond a purchase decision: helping ordinary people understand financial markets and plan their money with more confidence.

我希望 PauseBeforeBuy 未来不只回答“要不要买”，也能帮助普通人理解金融市场、整理自己的财务，让规划不再是一件遥远或难懂的事。

如果有一天这个项目产生收益，我希望把自己从中获得的收益全部用于公益。也希望在未来加入自愿参与的公益选项，先尊重每个人的现金流和意愿，让善意发生在有余力的时候。

A personal ambition, still ahead of me.
